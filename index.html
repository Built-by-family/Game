    How to play

    Get out before 3:00

    Finish five secret jobs around the school (they're different every game, so check your checklist), then escape through the front doors in the lobby. If a staff member grabs you, they drag you back to your desk. Three catches and it's detention. If the 3:00 bell rings first, you're staying after school.
